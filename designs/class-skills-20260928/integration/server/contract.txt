UpgradeSkillRequest: skill_table_id=1 uint32，expected_rank=2 uint32，count=3 uint32(只收1或10)。
UpgradeSkillResponse: error_message=1 TipInfoMessage，state=2 ListSkillsResponse，upgraded_count=3 uint32，stop_reason=4 string。
